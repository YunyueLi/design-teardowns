拆解画廊Colophon

Every exhibit in this gallery is made the same way: a headless browser reads the live site's computed styles and design tokens; assets, stylesheets and script sources are archived in full and read line by line; the study is then written in the subject's own visual language, as an interactive teardown and five companion documents (deconstruction, replication guide, sources and methods, fact check, design review). 馆里所有展品都用同一套方法制作：无头浏览器读取原站真实的计算样式和设计 token；资产、样式和脚本源代码全部存档并逐行精读；再按原作自己的视觉语言，写成互动拆解页和五份文档（设计解构、复刻指南、出处与方法、事实核查、设计评审）。
